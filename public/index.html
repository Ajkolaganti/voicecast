// how it works

Three steps. Done.

01

Upload your audio

Tap the upload zone and pick your file, or drag and drop it. Supports .m4a, .wav, .mp3, .ogg, .flac, and .aac.

02

Choose your settings

Pin a language or let Whisper auto-detect. Select a model tier: Tiny for speed, Large for accuracy.

03

Get your transcript

Results in under 5 seconds for short clips. Copy or download as .txt. Word count and language metadata included.
